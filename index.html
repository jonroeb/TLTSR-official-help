The Game

What is TLTSR?

The Legend of the Shattered Realm, commonly called TLTSR, is a sandbox adventure game built around exploration, survival, crafting, combat, procedural world generation, and progression.

The player begins in a generated world and gradually becomes stronger by gathering resources, crafting equipment, exploring biomes, discovering structures, and finding special progression items.

TLTSR is not designed around staying in one location. Exploration is one of the game's central mechanics.

Core Philosophy Explore first. Discover what the world contains. Progression is built around connecting discoveries together.

Sky Islands

A gateway above the ordinary world. The Sky Island is one of the most important locations in TLTSR's main progression. It is located high above the normal terrain and contains a special shrine and portal.

Reaching the island becomes significantly easier once the player has access to Wings.

Important Location Find the shrine on the Sky Island. The portal there is the gateway to the Shattered Blue Realm.

Enchanting Crystals

Enemies

Hostile creatures of the realm. Different enemies can appear in different areas of the world. Combat becomes increasingly important as the player enters more dangerous areas.

19

Azure Warden

Guardian of the Shattered Blue Realm.

◈

The Azure Warden

A major boss associated with the Shattered Blue Realm and one of TLTSR's major progression challenges.

The Azure Warden should not be approached as an ordinary enemy. Preparation matters.

Boss Strategy Survive first. Learn the attack pattern. Recognize openings. Strike when it is safe, then return to a safe position.

Recommended Preparation: Strong equipment, a reliable weapon, adequate survival supplies, good mobility, and knowledge of the surrounding terrain.

The Shattered Blue Realm

The world beyond the portal. The Shattered Blue Realm is a major advanced area reached through the special portal located at the Sky Island shrine.

1. Explore the Ordinary World

Build your equipment and discover progression items.

2. Obtain an Enchanting Crystal

The crystal is required to activate the portal.

3. Find the Sky Island

Search high above the normal terrain.

4. Open the Portal

Use the crystal at the shrine.

5. Enter the Blue Realm

The next major stage of the journey begins.

21

Blue Plains

The open lands of the Blue Realm. Blue Plains are a relatively flat region of the Shattered Blue Realm. The landscape differs visually and geographically from the normal world.

The Plains provide an open environment for exploration before the terrain becomes more mountainous.

22

Blue Hills

The mountainous region of the Blue Realm. The Blue Hills are a mountainous region of the Shattered Blue Realm. Their terrain reaches significantly higher elevations than surrounding areas.

Mobility Recommended Wings are especially useful here. Use vertical mobility to cross difficult terrain and reach elevated locations.
23

World Generation

How TLTSR creates its worlds. TLTSR uses procedural generation to dynamically create worlds. This allows terrain and world features to vary between seeds.

Terrain

Landscapes can vary between generated worlds.

Biomes

Different environmental regions are generated throughout the world.

Structures

Special locations can appear throughout generated worlds.

24

Seeds

The foundation of generated worlds. A seed determines the generated world. Seed-based generation makes it possible to share interesting worlds, revisit layouts, compare generation, and test unusual discoveries.

25

Multiplayer

Explore the realm together. TLTSR supports multiplayer through PeerJS. Players can experience the generated world together, exploring, building, gathering resources, fighting enemies, and progressing as a group.

Multiplayer Tech TLTSR's multiplayer system uses PeerJS for player connections.
26

Inventory

Keep the important stuff. The inventory stores resources, equipment, weapons, and other items. Inventory management becomes increasingly important during long exploration trips.

27

Minimap

Know where you are. TLTSR includes a minimap to help players understand their surroundings and navigate the generated world.

It becomes particularly useful when exploring unfamiliar terrain or traveling long distances.
